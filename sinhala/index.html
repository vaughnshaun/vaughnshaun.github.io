One course, several ways to learn

Build a routine that fits how you study.

01

A guided course

Follow graded lessons that mix vocabulary, grammar, conversation, sentence building, and immersion-script practice.

02

Focused study routes

Use dedicated word drills, searchable stories, topic lessons, grammar reference, and spaced repetition whenever you want extra practice.

03

Sinhala script with support

Study in Sinhala script, optionally mix in transliteration, reveal translations only when needed, and tap words for contextual meanings.

04

Complete offline access

After the first successful online load, the app caches its lessons, data, and linked study documents for use without an internet connection.

Keep learning anywhere

Install the app in a few taps.

Apple

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the app in Safari and wait for Offline ready.
  2. Tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Sinhala from the new Home Screen icon once while still online.

Android

Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome and wait for Offline ready.
  2. Use Install app or Add to Home screen from Chrome's menu.
  3. Launch the installed app once while still online.

Computer

Desktop

Open the app in a supporting browser such as Chrome or Edge, then use the browser's install control in the address bar or menu.

Your study data stays on your device. Progress, settings, and translation edits are stored in that browser and do not sync automatically. Clearing site data can remove both progress and offline files.
